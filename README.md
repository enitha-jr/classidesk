## ClassiDesk

ClassiDesk is an AI-powered ticket classification and management system for banking support operations that automatically routes customer tickets to the appropriate teams.

---

### Key Features

- AI-based ticket classification to automatically suggest the correct support team
- Create and manage tickets
- Track complete ticket lifecycle and ticket flow history
- Forward tickets between teams when required
- Resolve tickets with status tracking
- AI-powered chatbot to assist users before raising tickets
- Redis-based chat history persistence for faster responses
- API rate limiting to prevent abuse of AI services
- Email notifications when tickets are resolved

---

### Support Teams

- Digital Banking  
- Cards & Payments  
- Loans & EMI  
- Accounts & KYC  
- Fraud & Security  
- General Support
